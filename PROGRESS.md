- **Concept check:** the constraint nulls only `conversation_id` because deleting a conversation must keep the audit rows (principle 7: audit persistence) while detaching them from the deleted conversation; `user_id` must stay because it is the row's owner — nulling it would orphan the row from its owner and break RLS, and deleting the user already cascades and removes all of that user's rows anyway.
